Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

In short: OnePunto does not collect, send or share personal data. Everything you record stays on your device.

What the app stores

OnePunto stores your goal (its name, dates and the days that count), the days you have completed and your settings (theme, language, haptics and reminder). All of it is kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

What we do not do

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up.
  • There are no servers: the app does not connect to the internet.
  • There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We include no third-party tools that collect data.
  • The app asks for no access to your location, contacts or photos. It only asks to show notifications, and only if you turn on the daily reminder.

We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

Reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, OnePunto schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose, only on the days that count and only while today's dot is still open. It is created and shown on the phone itself, without going through any server. You can turn it off at any time in Settings or in the system's notification settings.

Backups

In Settings, if you tap “Copy to clipboard”, the app places your data as text on your device's clipboard. What you do with that text next, such as keeping it in your notes or emailing it to yourself, is up to you and the apps you choose. Restoring a backup replaces the app's data with it; if the stored data could not be read, a local copy is kept first so it can still be recovered.

System backups

If your device backup is turned on, such as Google backup on Android or iCloud on iPhone, the system may include OnePunto's data so you do not lose it when you change phones. That backup is handled by the system according to your settings; we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your goal inside the app. Uninstalling OnePunto makes the system remove all of its data from the device.

Children

OnePunto is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its date before releasing the change.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to hola@onepunto.com.
